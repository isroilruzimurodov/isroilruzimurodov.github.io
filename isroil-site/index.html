About

I was born in Samarkand, Uzbekistan, and moved to the United States to study. I am now pursuing an Associate of Science in Computer Science at the Borough of Manhattan Community College, part of the City University of New York.

Most of my work sits where technology meets community. I build software for Central Asian immigrants in New York, advise Uzbek-speaking students on university admissions and F-1 visas, and organize volunteers for national and international events back home.

Work & Projects

Karvon

Founder · Rental marketplace for Central Asian communities in NYC

A two-sided marketplace that helps newcomers find housing and rentals through people who speak their language. I designed, built, and shipped the MVP on my own, then set up production infrastructure and ran the first rounds of outreach to early users.
